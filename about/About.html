About me




bah! Mini me!


I’m a visual communication and industrial design major. I was born in 2006 and raised in Vacaville before (somewhat) moving to San Francisco for school. I’m a massive nerd; I love comics, video games, drawing, and really anything science fiction. Whether it’s drawing, painting, sculpting, or building, I love to work with my hands. If not for design, I’d love to be a comic book artist one day.

I’ve loved art and design my whole life and would love to pursue a life anywhere in that regard for a living (although though action figures would be an utter dream)! I’ve never really stuck to one thing, and I love to learn more. History? New techniques and viewpoints? You name it. Web design is another new tool I’d kill to have on the toolbelt. I hope from this class I walk away with at least one new skill that will be fun now and hopefully extremely helpful in the future.

Reflection


This was pretty smooth sailing. Though I will say, the biggest hurdle was the video portrait. Mostly issues with links, but that was all. I ended up going with how I usually represent myself in my own work. It’s what I’m most comfortable with, and what I had on hand. It paints me as more friendly, and also puts people off their guard before I present my work. Contrast, if you will. Regarding the biography, I wasn’t sure if we wanted something wikipedia style, or a genuine “about me”. So, I made a choice to stay informal. I’m fine with how it is, but I’d love to spruce this up.
